🎯 Executive Synthesis

Cloudflare (NET) is executing a bold "AI-first, agentic" transformation in 2026, cutting 20% of workforce to fund AI infrastructure while maintaining 34% revenue growth. The connectivity cloud model - combining network services, developer platform, and security - creates a unique compounding moat: every developer on Workers expands the network, every enterprise on Zero Trust deepens the relationship. Q1 2026 showed inflection: $639.8M revenue (+34% YoY), first $1B+ quarterly run-rate, 4,416 large customers (+25%), NRR >115%. Key watch items: FY2026 margin trajectory post-restructuring, AI revenue contribution (Workers AI, AI Gateway), and whether platformization can sustain >30% growth at $2.8B+ scale. Valuation at ~$45B market cap (~16x FY2026 revenue) prices in execution but leaves upside if AI becomes a meaningful revenue driver.
