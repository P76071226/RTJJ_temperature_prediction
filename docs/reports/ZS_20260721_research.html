🎯 Executive Synthesis

Zscaler (ZS) is the category-defining leader in cloud-delivered Zero Trust security (SSE/SASE). Unlike legacy firewall/VPN vendors who retrofit appliances, Zscaler built a global, multi-tenant proxy cloud from scratch - inspecting 300B+ requests/day with 9M+ SSL inspections/second. This architectural advantage creates a compounding data moat: every new customer improves threat detection for all. Business model is pure-play SaaS: per-user subscriptions ($72-375+/user/yr) with 81% gross margins and expanding operating leverage (22% non-GAAP op margin). ARR > $3B, NRR ~120%+ proves land-and-expand works. Customers are distributed enterprises (7,700+, 400+ Global 2000) replacing VPNs and securing cloud migration. Federal certifications (FedRAMP High, DoD IL5) create a high barrier in public sector. Why they win: (1) Cloud-native proxy architecture vs. lifted appliances, (2) Single-vendor platform breadth (ZIA+ZPA+ZDX+CASB+DLP), (3) Data scale to AI advantage, (4) Deep ecosystem integration + high switching costs. Risk: Valuation premium, competition from Microsoft (Entra), Palo Alto (Prisma), Netskope; macro slowdown in enterprise IT spend.

💰 How They Make Money

Zscaler operates a cloud-native Zero Trust security platform delivered as a service (SSE/SASE). Revenue comes from per-user, per-year subscriptions across two core product lines: 1. Zscaler Internet Access (ZIA) - Secure Web Gateway, CASB, DLP, Firewall-as-a-Service, Sandbox. Priced $72-325+/user/year (Essentials to Business to Transformation tiers). 2. Zscaler Private Access (ZPA) - Zero Trust Network Access (ZTNA) replacing VPNs. Priced $140-375+/user/year. Add-ons: Zscaler Digital Experience (ZDX) ~$2-5/user/mo, Workload Protection, Posture Control. Business model: High-margin SaaS with ~81% gross margins, expanding operating leverage (22% non-GAAP operating margin in Q4 FY24). ARR > $3B (crossed $2.5B in Q4 FY24, guided to $3B+). Land-and-expand: start with ZIA, upsell ZPA, ZDX, workloads.

👥 Who Their Customers Are

Target: Distributed enterprises (mid-market to Fortune 500) with hybrid work, cloud migration, and legacy VPN/firewall pain. 💲 Pricing Model

Model: Per-user, per-year subscription with tiered editions (Essentials to Business to Transformation). Price bands (annual per user): - ZIA: $72 (Essentials) to $90 (Business) to $325+ (Transformation) - ZPA: $140 to $375+ - Bundled ZIA+ZPA: ~$468 (government schedule) - Add-ons: ZDX $24-60/user/yr, Workloads ~$30K/yr base Discounting: Volume tiers - 34-35% per-user discount at 1,000+ seats. First-year TCO typically 1.3-1.5x subscription (professional services, training, internal resources). No public list pricing - all deals via direct sales/channel. Government GSA schedule provides transparency.

🏰 Why They Win (Moat)

1. Architectural Moat - Cloud-Native Proxy Built from ground up as multi-tenant cloud proxy (not lifted-and-shifted appliance). Inspects all traffic (SSL/TLS) at line rate - 300B+ requests/day, 9M+ SSL inspections/sec. Competitors (Palo Alto, Fortinet, Cisco) retrofit legacy stacks. 2. Data/Scale Network Effect 300B+ daily transactions feed AI/ML models for threat detection, anomaly scoring, policy optimization. New customers instantly benefit from global threat intelligence. 3. Platform Breadth - Single-Vendor SASE ZIA + ZPA + ZDX + CASB + DLP + FWaaS + Sandbox on one platform. Eliminates point-product sprawl. Gartner Magic Quadrant Leader for SSE (5+ years). 4. Zero Trust Exchange - Least-Privilege by Default User-to-app (not network-to-network) segmentation. No lateral movement. Patent portfolio (300+ patents) on proxy architecture, SSL inspection, policy engine. 5. Switching Costs / Land-and-Expand Deep integration with IdP (Okta, Azure AD), SIEM, SOAR, endpoint (CrowdStrike, SentinelOne). 120%+ NRR proves expansion. 6. Federal/Regulatory Certifications FedRAMP High, DoD IL5, StateRAMP - high barrier for new entrants in public sector.
