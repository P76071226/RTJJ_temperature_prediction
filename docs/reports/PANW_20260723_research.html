💰 How They Make Money

Palo Alto Networks (PANW) is executing a bold **platformization strategy** — consolidating fragmented security point products onto three integrated platforms: **Network Security (NGFW)**, **Prisma (SASE/Cloud)**, and **Cortex (SecOps/XDR)**. Revenue comes from: 1. **Product subscriptions** (software licenses, support) — recurring, high-margin 2. **Hardware appliances** (PA-Series NGFW: $2K-$400K) — lower margin, enables software attach 3. **Professional services** — deployment, optimization, incident response **Key metrics**: Next-Gen Security ARR **$4.2B+** (+43% YoY), **1,100+ platformized customers** added in Q3 FY26 (target: 4,000+ by FY30). Free product trials (e.g., Cortex XSIAM) drive adoption → long-term lock-in. Revenue growth re-accelerated to **+15.8% YoY**. RPO growth **+24%** outpacing revenue. Market cap ~$120B, largest pure-play cybersecurity vendor.
